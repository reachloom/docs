All tools act inside the workspace attached to the current key or OAuth connection. Tools prepare work but cannot send or approve email.

## Workspace and campaigns

| Tool | Inputs | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `get_status` | None | Product profile, Gmail status, campaign counts, email states |
| `list_campaigns` | None | Campaign IDs, brief, on/off state, review setting, and `canBeChangedHere` |
| `create_campaign` | `name`, `kind`, `ask` | Paused campaign requiring review, with `searchTaskId` if a search started |
| `update_brief` | `campaignId`; optional `ask`, `target`, `angle`, `exclusions` | Updated brief fields |

`kind` is `docs`, `list`, `call`, or `custom`. A campaign name is 1–60 characters and its ask is at most 300 characters. Brief parts are at most 500 characters each. Give the full replacement text of every part you update.

## Pages and searches

| Tool | Inputs | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `list_pages` | `campaignId` | Up to 200 pages with title, contact, email, status, and fit |
| `add_pages` | `campaignId`, `urls` | Add up to 50 web addresses; no contact email input |
| `find_leads` | `campaignId`; optional `count`, `focus` | `taskId`, or an existing running task with `alreadyRunning: true` |
| `get_task` | `taskId` | Status, total, result, and error |

`count` is 10, 20, or 30 and defaults to 10. `focus` is at most 300 characters. See [background tasks](/api/jobs) for polling.

## Emails

| Tool | Inputs | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `write_emails` | `campaignId`; optional `count` | Drafting summary; count is 1–10, default 5 |
| `list_emails` | None | Up to 40 recently updated emails, with IDs, kinds, states, bodies, and errors |
| `edit_draft` | `emailId`; optional `subject`, `body` | Saves a draft; queued emails are taken back first |
| `take_back_email` | `emailId` | Returns a queued email to a draft |

`edit_draft` needs at least one field. Subject is at most 300 characters and body at most 6,000. Drafting is also limited by daily capacity and workspace access.

## Conversations

| Tool | Inputs | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `check_inbox` | None | Syncs tracked Gmail conversations for new replies |
| `list_conversations` | None | Up to 60 conversations, with stage, intent, latest text, and `needsReply` |
| `read_conversation` | `conversationId` | Messages and waiting answers; each message text is limited to 2,000 characters |
| `draft_reply` | `conversationId`, `body` | Saves an answer as a draft and returns its `emailId` |

A reply body is 10–5,000 characters and must include the whole message. It goes through the draft checks. `draft_reply` refuses won or archived conversations and does not replace an answer written or approved by a person.

## Restrictions and limits

`update_brief`, `add_pages`, `find_leads`, and `write_emails` refuse a campaign that is on and sends without review. Pause it or enable **Review before sending** in the app first.

| Action | Hourly limit per person and workspace |
| --- | --- |
| Create campaign | 20 |
| Add pages | 20 |
| Find leads | 10 |
| Write emails | 10 |
| Check inbox | 20 |

The endpoint also allows 120 requests per minute per person and workspace. [Billing limits](/account/plans), contact rules, and draft checks still apply. There are no tools for sending, approval, deleting, switching campaigns on, or managing settings.
