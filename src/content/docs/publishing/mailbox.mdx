Each workspace sends from one mailbox. Open **Settings → Mailbox** and choose **Connect mailbox**. All campaigns in the workspace send from it, in your name, and replies come back to it.

| Type | Best for | How you connect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Gmail](/publishing/gmail) | Google Workspace or a personal Gmail account | Sign in with Google and allow sending and reading replies |
| [Outlook / Microsoft 365](/publishing/outlook) | Outlook.com, Hotmail, or a work account | Sign in with Microsoft and allow sending and reading replies |
| [Other (SMTP + IMAP)](/publishing/smtp) | Zoho, Fastmail, iCloud, Yahoo, or your own domain | Enter a user name and an app password |

All three work the same after they are connected. Sending limits, the cancel delay, sending hours, and the protections in [review and sending](/publishing/sending) apply to every mailbox type.

## What Reachloom reads

Reachloom reads only the conversations started by its own sent emails. It does not import your inbox, whichever mailbox you connect. Replies are checked in the background, and **Check inbox** looks right away.

## Change the mailbox

A workspace has one mailbox at a time. To switch, disconnect the current one and connect another. Conversations already in the Inbox stay. A conversation started from the old mailbox may not get new replies after you disconnect it.

If the status says **Needs reconnecting**, connect the same mailbox again. Nothing is sent while the provider rejects the saved access.
