Open **Results** to see outreach over the last 90 days. The chart records observed activity by UTC date.

| Measure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Sent | Emails Gmail accepted, including replies and follow-ups |
| Replies | The first human reply in each conversation |
| Won | Conversations marked won, by you or a link check |

A sent email means Gmail accepted it. It does not prove that the person opened or read it. Automatic replies and bounce notices do not count as human replies.

## Link checks

Mention and list campaigns can reopen contacted pages to look for a link to your website. Finding one marks the conversation won. Checks cover emailed pages for up to 90 days, according to the campaign's [Automation](/tools/automation) settings.

Reachloom does not scan the whole web for backlinks. A win records the outcome in the conversation; it is not a search ranking or traffic measurement.
