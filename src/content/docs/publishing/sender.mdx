Open **Settings → Sender** to set who your emails are from and how they end.

## Name

The name people see in their inbox, and the `{name}` in your signature. Leave it empty to use the name of the person who created the workspace. Use a plain name on one line.

## Postal address

Commercial email must show where its sender can be reached by post. Add yours (up to 3 lines and 200 characters, written as plain text without links). Reachloom prints it above the opt-out line of every **first email and follow-up**.

**Nothing is sent until you add it.** First emails and follow-ups wait with the reason shown, and a campaign cannot be switched on without it. Answers inside a conversation do not carry the address.

## Signature

The lines under the message, with `{name}` and `{site}` filled in. Leave it empty for your name and your site. Keep it to a few lines of plain text. It cannot contain placeholders left unfilled, mentions of AI or a tool, or links to sites other than yours and your booking link.

## Footer: saying no

Every first email and follow-up ends with a line that asks the reader to reply "no". That line is always there. Below it you can show an **unsubscribe link** that stops every campaign for that address at once.

- The link opens a page that asks for confirmation. Opening it does nothing, so mail scanners cannot unsubscribe anyone by accident.
- Mail apps that offer their own Unsubscribe button get a one-click request as well, where the provider allows it.
- Turn the link off if you prefer replies only. The opt-out line stays.
- Word the line your way, in one line up to 200 characters. Put `{link}` where the link goes, or `{link:Click here}` to choose the words that are the link in the HTML version.

Someone who unsubscribes is added to [People](/tools/people) as **Do not contact**, and no campaign writes to them again.
