Open **Settings → Mailbox** in the workspace, choose **Connect mailbox**, and pick **Gmail**. All campaigns in that workspace send from this mailbox. To send from another kind of mailbox, see [choose a mailbox](/publishing/mailbox).

## Connect and test

1. Choose **Gmail** as the type and continue.
2. Sign in to the Google account you want to send from.
3. Allow sending and reading replies.
4. Return to Reachloom and check the connection status.
5. Send yourself a test email.

Reachloom signs campaign emails with the name of the person who connected Gmail. Check that name in the person's account profile.

If sending permission is missing, the connection is not saved. If reading permission is missing, emails can be sent but replies cannot appear in the Inbox. Connect again and allow both permissions.

## What Reachloom reads

Reachloom reads conversations started by its own sent emails. It does not import your whole Gmail inbox.

Replies sync in the background. Use **Check inbox** to look for new replies sooner.

## Reconnect or disconnect

If the status says **Needs reconnecting**, connect Gmail again. Nothing is sent while Google rejects the saved access.

Disconnecting removes the workspace's mailbox connection. Removing the member who connected Gmail also disconnects it, so another member must connect a mailbox before sending can continue.
