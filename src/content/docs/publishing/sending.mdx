Use a campaign's **Emails** menu to write first emails. Reachloom reads each eligible page and prepares a message using your product profile and campaign brief.

## Review drafts

Open **Inbox → Drafts** and choose an email. Check the recipient, subject, message, and reason the page fits. Edit anything that needs changing.

- **Approve** queues the email with a cancel delay, normally five minutes.
- **Take back** returns a queued email to a draft before it leaves.
- **Send now** sends directly after the contact, mailbox, billing, and daily limit checks.

Editing a queued email returns it to a draft. Failed sends are not retried automatically.

For campaigns that require review, emails wait until approved. With review off, the campaign can queue messages by itself while it is on. A paused campaign does not send queued emails automatically.

## Set the sending window

Open **Settings → Sending** to choose the workspace's time zone, working days, and start and end hours. These settings control automatic sends and scheduled campaign work.

Manual sends with **Send now** or the reply box can go out outside the window and while the campaign is paused. They still obey contact rules and daily limits.

## Daily limits and spacing

Each campaign and the workspace mailbox have a daily limit. Both default to 10 and can be set up to 50. The mailbox limit covers all campaigns together. Usage is checked over the last 24 hours.

Automatic first emails and follow-ups are normally at least five minutes apart across the mailbox. Answers in an existing conversation do not wait for that gap.

An email can remain queued after its delay if the mailbox, working hours, limits, or billing prevent sending. Check its reason and the campaign's [Activity](/tools/activity).

## Contact rules

Reachloom checks blocked addresses and domains again before sending. It also prevents another first email to the same address in the workspace, even if a campaign or page was deleted.

Opt-outs, bounces, and refusals stop further outreach to that address. Review them in [Exclusions](/account/exclusions).

## Address checks

Before an address is saved or an email is sent, Reachloom checks that it is written correctly and that its domain has mail records. It never contacts the mailbox itself.

- An address whose domain cannot receive email is not used. The page stays in **Needs email** and its notes say why. A typed address is refused with the reason. If a domain stops accepting mail after an email was drafted, the email is cancelled.
- A probable typo of a big provider, such as gmial.com, is flagged and can still be sent.
- If the lookup itself fails, the address is not blocked.

## Automatic pause

Many bounces damage a mailbox. If 8% or more of the emails sent in the last 7 days bounced (with at least 10 sent), or 5 emails bounced within a day, Reachloom switches off every campaign that sends from the workspace's mailbox. Each campaign's [Activity](/tools/activity) says why, and you get an alert if you have set up [notifications](/account/notifications).

Bounced addresses are already on your do-not-contact list. When you switch a campaign on again, Reachloom asks you to confirm and counts only new bounces. The workspace chat and MCP cannot switch a paused mailbox back on. Reachloom cannot see spam complaints through Gmail, Outlook, or SMTP, so bounces are the signal it uses.
