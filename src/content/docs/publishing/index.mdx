Reachloom sends through the mailbox connected to the workspace: Gmail, Outlook, or any mailbox with SMTP and IMAP. Campaigns can wait for review or send automatically, depending on their settings.

- [Choose a mailbox](/publishing/mailbox): [Gmail](/publishing/gmail), [Outlook](/publishing/outlook), or [another mailbox](/publishing/smtp), then test it.
- [Review and send](/publishing/sending) drafts, with daily limits and working hours.
- [Manage conversations](/publishing/conversations) in the Inbox.
- [Check results](/publishing/results) for sends, replies, and links marked won.

WordPress publishing and a content editor are not available yet. A booking link can be used for calls; Reachloom does not connect to your calendar.
