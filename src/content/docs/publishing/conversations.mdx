The **Inbox** brings together every campaign in the workspace. Filter by campaign when you want to focus on one.

First emails that have not been sent appear under **Drafts**. After a pitch is sent, its replies and follow-ups stay inside the conversation.

## Open a draft

Open **Inbox → Drafts** and choose an email. The panel shows:

- **Relations** at the top: a chip for the page and a chip for the campaign. The page chip opens that page inside its campaign. The campaign chip opens the campaign. Neither links to the other site's own address.
- **To**: a person chip. It opens the person in [People](/tools/people).
- **From**: your name and mailbox address, as `Name <email>`. It opens **Settings → Mailbox**.
- The subject and the message. Click a field to change it.

The green **Approve** button queues the email. Other actions are in the ⋮ menu (see [Review and send emails](/publishing/sending#review-drafts)).

## Read and answer

Click a row to open the conversation panel. Its page and campaign chips and its person chip work the same way as in a draft. Read the thread, check any proposed answer, and edit it before sending. You can also write an answer yourself.

Reachloom reads only the conversations started by its own sent emails, from whichever [mailbox](/publishing/mailbox) you connected. **Check inbox** looks for new replies now; background sync also runs regularly.

## Automatic replies

Campaigns can answer simple replies automatically when review is off. Interested answers can include a suggested edit for a mention. If someone wants a call, the answer can offer the booking link from **Settings → Sending**. Without a booking link, call answers wait for you.

Some replies need your attention. Check **Needs you** rather than assuming every answer was handled automatically. Bounce notices and automatic replies are not counted as human replies.

## Things for you to do

When someone says yes to adding your mention or link, you get a **They agreed** [notification](/account/notifications) and the conversation appears under **To do**. Reachloom sends nothing to them for this step: do whatever your part is, such as publishing your side or sending them the exact change.

Press **I did it** on the row or in the conversation panel to take it off the list. Marking the conversation **Won** also clears it. Only the first yes counts, so later messages do not add the conversation back.

## When someone says they added it

A reply that says your mention or link is up is treated as a claim, not a fact. Reachloom opens the page and looks. If it finds your link, the conversation is marked **Won** and no other answer is written. If it cannot find or read it, the answer says so (or that a look will follow) and waits for you, even in a campaign that answers by itself.

## Deleted conversations

Deleting a conversation moves it to the **Deleted** tab and takes back anything waiting to go out in it. Bring it back, or remove it for good. If the person writes again, the conversation returns to the Inbox. Closing a page has the same effect on its conversations.

## Close a conversation

Mark a conversation **Won** when the requested result happened. For a link, give the live page address. Mention and list campaigns can also mark it won when an automatic check finds the link.

Archive a conversation when you want to close it. A refusal, opt-out, or bounce also blocks further outreach to the address through [Exclusions](/account/exclusions).

Use [notifications](/account/notifications) to hear about replies, agreements, work that needs you, wins, and problems.
