Open **Settings → Members**. Everyone in a workspace is an admin, including invited members. There are no separate viewer or editor roles.

## Invite a person

1. Create an invite link.
2. Copy it and share it with the person you want to join.
3. They sign in, open the link, and choose **Join**.

Each link works once and expires after seven days. Opening it does not join the workspace until the person presses **Join**. You can cancel unused invites. A workspace can have up to 20 unused, unexpired links.

## Remove a member

Removing someone revokes their workspace API keys, agent sign-ins, unused invites, and personal email notification channel. If they connected Gmail, that mailbox is disconnected too.

If the creator leaves, the oldest remaining member becomes the creator. The last person cannot leave an empty workspace; delete the workspace instead.

All members can manage campaigns, connections, billing, and access. Share invite links only with people who should have those permissions.
