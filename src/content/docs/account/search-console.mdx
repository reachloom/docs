Open **Results → Search**. **Connect with Google** asks for read-only access to Search Console and nothing else. Reachloom cannot change anything in your property.

1. Connect with the Google account that can see your property.
2. Choose one of the properties Google lists for that account.
3. Pick 7, 28 or 90 days.

## What you see

Clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position, each compared with the period just before, a daily chart of clicks, and your top queries and pages. Pages that are also in your Content list show their title.

- Click-through rate is total clicks divided by total impressions, not an average of daily rates.
- Position is Google's own average: lower is better.
- Google leaves rare queries out for privacy, so the query list does not add up to the totals.
- Google's data is final about two days late, and a note says when a range is missing its newest days.
- Reachloom reads the numbers from Google when you open the tab and keeps them for a few minutes. It does not store them or send queries or page addresses anywhere else.

Search traffic moves for many reasons. These numbers do not show that a link you won caused a change.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** revokes the access at Google and deletes the saved token. If Google stops accepting the connection, or the person who connected is removed from the workspace, it disconnects and asks you to connect again.

This feature is switched on per server and stays off until Google has approved Reachloom's access. While it is off, the **Search** tab shows "Search Console is not available yet".
