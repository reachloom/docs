Open **Settings → Exclusions** to review blocked addresses and domains. These rules apply across the workspace's campaigns and are checked before an email is sent.

## Add or review a block

Add an email address or domain when you do not want outreach to reach it. Reachloom also adds exclusions for opt-outs, bounces, and refusals.

Blocking a person is done in [People](/tools/people), where each address appears once and the reason is shown. Exclusions here list blocked sites and addresses together. An exclusion differs from skipping a page. Skipping affects that page in a campaign; an exclusion stops outreach to the address or domain across the workspace.

## Lift an exclusion

Only a person in the dashboard can lift an exclusion. One added automatically needs confirmation. The workspace chat and MCP have no tool to do this.

Check why the address was blocked before lifting it. Removing an exclusion does not erase previous contact history or allow a second first email to an address already contacted.
