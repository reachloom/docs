Open **Settings → Notifications** to add a destination. A workspace can have up to 10 channels.

## Add a channel

Choose **Slack**, **Discord**, **Webhook**, or **Email**, enter the details requested, and send a test. Use a webhook address for the Slack or Discord channel you want to receive messages in. Email notifications go to the member's own address.

## Choose events

| Event | When it happens |
| --- | --- |
| They replied | A person answers, including interest, a question, a call request, or a refusal |
| Needs you | A reply needs attention or drafts wait for approval |
| Won | Your link is found or a conversation is marked won |
| Problems | Gmail needs reconnecting, sending fails, or Reachloom pauses a campaign |

Open a campaign's notification settings to choose its events and destinations. The default is every event on every channel. Workspace-wide problems can notify channels independently of a campaign's choices.

Notifications are delivered in the background. Check the Inbox and Activity for the full conversation and error details.
