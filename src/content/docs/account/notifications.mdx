Open **Settings → Notifications** to add a destination. A workspace can have up to 10 channels.

## Add a channel

Choose **Slack**, **Discord**, **Webhook**, or **Email**, enter the details requested, and send a test. Use a webhook address for the Slack or Discord channel you want to receive messages in. Email notifications go to the member's own address.

## Choose events

| Event | When it happens |
| --- | --- |
| They replied | A person answers, including interest, a question, a call request, or a refusal |
| They agreed | A person says yes to adding your mention or link, so there is something for you to do. The conversation appears under **To do** in the Inbox |
| Needs you | A reply needs attention or drafts wait for approval |
| Won | Your link is found or a conversation is marked won |
| Problems | the mailbox needs reconnecting, sending fails, or Reachloom pauses a campaign |

Open a campaign's notification settings to choose its events and destinations. The default is every event on every channel. A campaign that saved its own list of events before **They agreed** existed does not send it until you switch it on. Workspace-wide problems can notify channels independently of a campaign's choices.

Notifications are delivered in the background. Check the Inbox and Activity for the full conversation and error details.

## Updates and the weekly report

Each channel has an **Updates** switch, off by default. When it is on, the channel also hears about things that are not about one campaign:

| Update (`type`) | When |
| --- | --- |
| `content.discovered` | A new page was found from a feed or sitemap (not for the first import) |
| `campaign.recommended` | New campaign ideas are ready |
| `campaign.draft_created` | A paused draft was made from an idea |
| `backlink.status_changed` | A watched link was confirmed changed, gone, or back |
| `report.ready` | The weekly report |

The **weekly report** goes out on Monday at 09:00 in your workspace's time zone (Settings → Sending), for the previous Monday to Sunday, once. It exists only for workspaces with at least one channel that has **Updates** switched on. It counts pages found by searches, people reached with a first email, replies and positive replies, new links found, links lost, and what is waiting for you. It holds numbers and a link to Results only: no names, addresses or message text.

## Webhook format

The five events above keep the format they always had. Updates use an envelope with an id you can use to deduplicate:

```json
{
  "id": "evt_3f1c9a0b2d7e4a61b8c5d0f2",
  "type": "content.discovered",
  "version": 1,
  "created": "2026-11-28T08:00:00.000Z",
  "workspaceId": "…",
  "title": "New page found",
  "url": "https://my.reachloom.app/w/…/content",
  "data": { "assetId": "…", "url": "https://example.com/blog/post", "sourceId": "…" }
}
```

Every webhook is signed. The headers are `x-reachloom-timestamp` (seconds) and `x-reachloom-signature`: `sha256=` followed by the hex HMAC-SHA256 of `timestamp.body` with your secret. Reject a timestamp more than 5 minutes old. Answer with any 2xx status within 8 seconds. Anything else is tried again: 3 attempts in total, waiting 1 and then 2 minutes.

The [Webhooks](/api/webhooks) page has the full payloads, the event types, and code to check the signature in Node and Python.

## Secret, delivery log and sending again

- **Rotate signing secret** makes a new secret and shows it once. The old one stops working immediately.
- **Delivery log** lists the newest 20 messages of a channel with the answer your server gave (the HTTP status) and why a failed one failed. Older ones are deleted.
- **Send again** on a failed message sends that same message again with the same `id` and starts its attempts again. It works on failed messages only. Nothing else in Reachloom is repeated.

To open the log, right-click a channel (or use its ⋯ button) and choose **Delivery log**. In the demo, a failed delivery is sent again and then shows as Sent.
