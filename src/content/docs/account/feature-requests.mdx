**Settings → Integrations** lists what you can connect today and, under **Soon**, things we could build. Campaign types, reports and developer tools are listed the same way where they belong, such as in **New campaign** and **Results**.

A **Request** button tells us you would use something. It is a signal of interest. It is not a promise to build it, there is no date, and you will not be notified. Nothing under **Soon** works yet.

After you press it the button says **Requested on this browser**. That is kept in a small cookie on your browser for 90 days, so it does not send twice. It is not shared between devices or accounts, and clearing cookies clears it. If analytics is switched off for your installation, the button says so instead of claiming your interest was recorded.

## Analytics

While you are signed in, Reachloom uses Google Analytics to count which screens and features are used and which features are asked for. It receives the name of the screen (for example "campaign brief"), the feature involved and whether your workspace is on a paid plan. It never receives email addresses, names, people you write to, workspace or campaign names, or message text. Details are in the [privacy policy](https://reachloom.app/privacy/). Signing in means agreeing to the terms on the sign-in page.
