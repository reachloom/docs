Open **Settings → Billing** in a workspace for its current status, usage, and checkout price. Each workspace has its own subscription. Any member can manage it.

## Preview and subscription

When billing is enabled, a workspace that has never paid can try lead search and drafting without sending emails.

| Access | Found pages | Drafts | New people contacted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview | 20 total | 5 total | None |
| Subscription | 2,000 per billing period | No separate plan limit | 300 per billing period |

Follow-ups and replies do not count as new people contacted. Daily campaign and mailbox limits still apply, including when a plan has remaining contacts.

The preview is a total allowance, not a monthly grant. Deleting a campaign or page does not reset usage or let the workspace send another first email to an address already contacted.

## Manage billing

Use the billing page to subscribe or open the billing portal for payment details and cancellation. If cancellation is scheduled for the end of the period, access continues until then.

A failed payment has a seven-day grace period before sending stops. After a subscription ends, automatic work stops. You can still answer existing conversations by hand for 30 days after it ended.

## When billing is not enabled

Some installations run with billing off or with a free workspace allowance. The billing page shows that state, and subscription quotas do not apply there. Sending limits and contact rules still apply.
