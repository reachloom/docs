Reachloom has two ways to work with AI alongside the dashboard.

- [Workspace chat](/ai/chat) can help with campaigns, searches, emails, and conversations. Some actions, including queuing emails, need a direct request from you.
- [MCP](/ai/mcp) connects your own AI tool to one workspace. It can read and prepare work, but cannot send emails or switch campaigns on.

See [what an agent can do](/ai/mcp-capabilities) before connecting a client, or try an [example workflow](/ai/workflows).

To connect n8n, Zapier, or Make, see [Automation recipes](/ai/automation-recipes). They call `/mcp` over HTTP and receive [signed webhooks](/api/webhooks).

AI searches and writing use the same workspace [usage limits](/account/plans) as the dashboard. Check product facts and drafts before using them.
