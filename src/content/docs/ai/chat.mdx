Open the chat button in your workspace. The conversation belongs to that workspace, so the assistant has the product profile, campaign status, and outreach context.

## Ask for work

> Create a campaign asking for a place on a list of uptime tools. Keep it paused.

> Find 10 more pages for this campaign, focused on tools for small teams.

> Write five first emails and show me the drafts.

> Check the Inbox and tell me which conversations need an answer.

The chat can show a plan for work with several steps. Progress stays with the answer, and longer searches also appear in the AI progress window.

## Actions that affect outreach

The workspace chat can queue emails when you directly ask it to send or approve them. It can also help pause or start campaigns, write replies, and manage conversation outcomes when asked. Read the result and check the Inbox or campaign afterward.

Queued emails use the cancel delay and sending checks. You can take them back before they leave. The chat cannot lift an exclusion; do that yourself in **Settings → Exclusions**.

## Chat and MCP

An outside agent connected through MCP has fewer permissions than the workspace chat. It can prepare campaigns and drafts, but cannot approve, send, switch a campaign on, or close conversations. See [MCP capabilities](/ai/mcp-capabilities).
