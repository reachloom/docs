A **workspace** is one product or site. Its product description, mailbox, campaigns, conversations, members, and billing stay together.

A **campaign** has one ask, such as getting a mention in a blog post. It has its own brief, pages, automation, and activity.

A **page** is a web address you may want to contact. It holds the contact name, email, reason it fits, status, and notes. A sent email starts a conversation in the Inbox.

## Add a workspace

Open **All workspaces**, choose **New workspace**, and follow the setup questions. Reachloom can read your website and suggest a name, description, and notes. Review these before finishing: future emails use them.

You can skip reading the website and fill in the details yourself. Change them later in **Settings → General**.

Use the workspace switcher to move between products. Check the selected workspace before connecting a mailbox or creating a campaign.

## Find your work

The main sidebar has **Inbox**, **People**, **Campaigns**, **Content**, and **Results**. **Settings** is a button above them, next to Home and the chat button. **Content** opens to **Pages**, **Ideas**, and **Sources**: it watches your own site and is where campaign ideas come from. A campaign has its own **Pages**, **Brief**, **Automation**, and **Activity** screens.

Click a record to open its side panel. Most detail fields become editable when you click them; leaving the field saves, and Escape cancels. Table rows also have a right-click menu for their actions.

[Invite members](/account/members) when someone else needs access. Every member is an admin of that workspace.
