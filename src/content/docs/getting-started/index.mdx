Reachloom helps you find pages that fit your product, write to the people behind them, and follow the conversation. A campaign can ask for a mention, a place on a list, a call, or something else.

Work in the dashboard, ask the [workspace chat](/ai/chat), or connect your own agent through [MCP](/ai/mcp).

## Your first campaign

1. [Open Reachloom](https://my.reachloom.app) and sign in.
2. Create a workspace for your product or site. Add its website, then check the description Reachloom suggests.
3. Open **Campaigns** and create a campaign. Choose what each email should ask for and who it should reach.
4. Choose **I approve each email** if you want to review every message before it goes out.
5. Check the first lead search on the campaign's **Pages** screen. Add contact details where needed.
6. [Connect a mailbox](/publishing/mailbox) (Gmail, Outlook, or another), then write emails from the campaign's **Emails** menu.
7. Open **Inbox**, review the drafts, and approve the ones you want to send. Switch the campaign on for queued emails to go out.

Approval queues an email with a delay. **Send now** sends it directly after the same contact, mailbox, and limit checks. See [review and sending](/publishing/sending).

When billing is enabled, the preview lets you find 20 pages and write five drafts. You need a subscription to send. Check [billing and usage](/account/plans).
