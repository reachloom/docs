Your product profile tells Reachloom what you offer and who it helps. Open **Settings → General** to check the workspace name, website, description, and notes.

## Write the description

Say what the product does and who it is for. Use facts a person can check on your website. For example:

> A simple uptime monitor for small teams. It checks websites and sends an email when they stop responding.

Use the notes for useful details, limits, or things the emails should avoid saying. Check AI suggestions before saving them.

## Give each campaign a clear ask

The product profile is shared across the workspace. The [campaign brief](/tools/brief) says what that campaign asks for and who it should contact.

Keep one ask per campaign. A request for a mention and a request for a call usually need different pages and different messages.

For calls, add your booking link in **Settings → Sending**. Reachloom uses the link in answers when someone wants a call; it does not connect to your calendar.
