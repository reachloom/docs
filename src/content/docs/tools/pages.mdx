Open a campaign's **Pages** screen. Each row holds a page address, contact details, why it fits, status, and notes. Mention and list campaigns also have a **Where it fits** field.

## Add pages

Use **Add pages** to paste web addresses or import a CSV file. You can paste one address per line, or separate addresses with spaces or commas. Duplicate addresses in the same campaign are skipped.

Use these CSV headers:

```csv
url,email,name,why_fit,status,notes
https://example.com/resources,hello@example.com,Alex,Lists tools for small teams,new,Check the latest entries
```

`url` identifies the page. Contact details are optional. A page without an email starts as **Needs email**. You can also [find leads](/tools/find-leads) instead of importing a list.

## Edit and organize

Click a row to open its side panel and edit fields. Use the status tabs to see **To do**, **Ready**, **Sent**, **Replied**, **Linked**, or **Skipped** pages. Search, filters, sorting, and column controls help with larger lists.

Row menus and selection actions let you skip or delete pages. Skipping keeps the record while leaving it out of outreach. Deleting a page does not reset the workspace's record of people already contacted.

## Find an address or write emails

Use **Emails** to look for contact addresses or write drafts for eligible pages. Reachloom opens pages to find addresses; it does not use an address invented by AI. If no email is found, the panel may show a contact form, author page, or profile instead.

Use the export action to download a CSV of the list. Review the addresses and notes before sharing it.
