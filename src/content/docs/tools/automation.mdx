Open a campaign and choose **Automation**. The flow is fixed; click a step to change its settings and save the panel.

## Configure the flow

| Step | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| Find pages | Automatic searches, interval, pages per search, instructions, and excluded sites |
| Find an email | Whether shared inboxes such as `hello@` are allowed |
| Check the fit | Competitors and other reasons to skip a page |
| Write the email | Writing instructions |
| Review and send | Review before sending, campaign daily limit, and cancel delay |
| They reply | Whether simple replies are answered automatically, and reply instructions |
| No reply | Whether to send one follow-up, its delay, and instructions |
| Link appears | Whether and how often to check emailed pages for your link |
| Say thanks | Whether to send a short thank-you after a conversation is won |

Automatic searches default to 10 pages at most every 24 hours. You can choose 10, 20, or 30 pages and an interval from 6 to 168 hours.

## Review and sending

Turn on **Review before sending** to hold messages for approval. Automatic answers to simple replies need this setting to be off as well as **Answer simple replies by itself** to be on.

The campaign and mailbox each default to 10 emails a day, with a maximum of 50. Both limits apply. Queued emails use a cancel delay, normally five minutes, before they can leave. See [sending](/publishing/sending) for working hours and manual sends.

## Follow-ups and links

By default, a conversation can receive one follow-up after four quiet days. You can turn this off or choose a delay from 2 to 14 days. A new reply stops a pending follow-up.

Mention and list campaigns can check emailed pages for your link for up to 90 days. The default interval is three days; you can choose 1 to 30 days. Finding the link marks the conversation won. This checks the contacted page, not the whole web.

## The thank-you

When a conversation is marked won, the campaign can send one short thank-you in the same thread. It is on by default and can be turned off for each campaign. The text is fixed, not written by AI, and it is the only email that says an AI assistant wrote the emails and points to reachloom.app. It follows the campaign's review and delay settings, and it is never sent to someone on your do-not-contact list.

## Test without sending

Use the flow's page test to see how a page would be handled and what email would be written. The test does not save or send an email.

## Protections

Opt-outs, blocked contacts, one first email per address in the workspace, the opt-out line, AI draft checks, the address check, and automatic pausing when emails bounce always apply. They cannot be switched off in the flow.
