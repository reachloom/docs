Open a campaign's **Pages** screen and choose **Find leads**, the sparkle button beside sort.

## Start a search

1. Choose 10, 20, or 30 pages.
2. Add a focus if this search should be narrower than the brief.
3. Start the search and follow its progress.
4. Review the pages added to the campaign.

Reachloom searches the web, opens candidate pages, checks whether they fit, and looks for a contact. Duplicate, excluded, unavailable, or unsuitable pages can be rejected, so a search may add fewer pages than requested.

Contact emails must appear on pages Reachloom opened. A named person is preferred. Shared inboxes such as `hello@` can be allowed in **Automation → Find an email**. Support, legal, donation, and newsletter addresses are not used.

## Follow progress

The AI progress window shows the current work and results. Minimize it to keep using the dashboard, then reopen it from the bottom of the sidebar. Lead searches, contact searches, and email drafting continue on the server.

A page without a usable address stays in **Needs email**. Open it to check any contact form or profile found, add an address yourself, or run the email search again.

## Search automatically

In [Automation](/tools/automation), enable **Find new pages by itself**. It searches when few pages are waiting, while the campaign is on and within its working hours. The default is at most once every 24 hours, with 10 pages per search.

[Billing limits](/account/plans) also apply to pages found through automatic searches, chat, and MCP.
