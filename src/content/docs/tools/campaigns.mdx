Open **Campaigns** and start a new campaign. The setup asks what every email should ask for, who to look for, why they would care, and who to leave alone.

## Choose an ask

| Kind | What the campaign asks for |
| --- | --- |
| Mention in a blog post | A mention with a link in an existing article |
| Add to a list | A place in a maintained list, resource page, or directory |
| Book a call | A short call with someone who may use your product |
| Something else | Your own request |

AI can suggest answers from your product profile. Edit them before finishing. Keep the ask specific and short.

## Choose how it runs

| Mode | Search | Sending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fully automatic | Finds new pages by itself | Sends emails and answers simple replies by itself |
| I approve each email | Finds new pages by itself | Waits for your approval |
| I start searches myself | You start Find leads | Sends emails and answers simple replies by itself |

**I start searches myself** only changes how searches start. Choose **I approve each email** if you want messages to wait for review.

The setup can start the campaign when you finish. Check this choice before saving. If billing does not allow sending, the campaign stays paused. A new campaign also starts a search for 20 pages when AI and usage limits allow it.

## Change it later

Open the campaign to edit its [Brief](/tools/brief) and [Automation](/tools/automation). Pause it when you want automatic work to stop. You can still review drafts while it is paused.

The workspace chat creates campaigns paused. Campaigns created through MCP are also paused and require review before sending.
