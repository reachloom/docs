Open **People** in the sidebar for everyone in the workspace with an email address: the people behind your pages and the people you have written to. Each address appears once, however many campaigns and pages it appears in.

## What you can see

Use the tabs to narrow the list:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| All | Everyone, with a status |
| Replied | People who wrote back |
| Waiting | People who got a first email and have not answered |
| Do not contact | People who must not be written to, and why |

Click a person to open their panel. It shows their pages and conversations across campaigns. The same panel opens from the Inbox and from a page.

## Add someone by hand

Choose **New person** and enter an email address, and optionally a name and a note. The address is checked first: if its domain cannot receive email, it is refused with the reason. You can then add a page for that person or [write an email on a page](/tools/pages#write-an-email-on-a-page).

## Do not contact

A person can be marked **Do not contact**. It applies to every campaign in the workspace and is checked again before each email is sent. Reachloom adds it for you when someone asks to stop, when an address bounces, and when someone says they are not interested. The reason is shown next to the status: *Asked to stop*, *Bounced*, *Not interested* or *Added by you*.

To write to someone again, choose **Allow emails again**. Reachloom warns you first when it had blocked them itself, because emailing someone who asked to stop can break anti-spam and privacy law. Blocking a whole site is done in [Exclusions](/account/exclusions).

Allowing a person again never allows a second **first** email to the same address: Reachloom keeps one first email per address in the workspace, even when pages or campaigns are deleted.

## Delete a person

You can delete someone who is not blocked and has no conversations. Their pages stay, without the address, and anything waiting to be sent to them is taken back. A blocked person cannot be deleted, because that would lift the block. If they have conversations, delete those for good in the Inbox under **Deleted** first. The record of who was emailed is kept, so the address is still never emailed twice.
