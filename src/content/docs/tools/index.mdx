A campaign brings together the ask, the people you want to reach, and the work needed to contact them.

| Guide | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| [Create a campaign](/tools/campaigns) | Choose an ask and a run mode |
| [Campaign brief](/tools/brief) | Explain who to contact and why they would care |
| [Pages and CSV](/tools/pages) | Add, edit, import, and export pages |
| [Find leads](/tools/find-leads) | Search for fitting pages and contact details |
| [Automation](/tools/automation) | Set search, sending, reply, and follow-up behavior |
| [People](/tools/people) | See everyone with an address, and who must not be contacted |
| [Activity](/tools/activity) | Check what happened and why work is waiting |

Campaigns share the workspace's mailbox and daily sending limit. Each campaign also has its own limit. See [sending settings](/publishing/sending).
